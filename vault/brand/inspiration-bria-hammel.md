# Summer's inspiration: Bria Hammel Interiors

Summer loves https://briahammelinteriors.com/ (especially `/portfolio/`) and
wants MDB's site to feel similar. Studied 2026-10-05. When she says "like
Bria", "more Bria", "make it feel like that site", this is what she means.

**Borrow the feeling and the layout moves. Never copy their photos, words,
logo or exact colours.** Translate everything into MDB's colours and fonts
(`brand-guide.md`).

## The feeling
Calm, airy, editorial, "luxury but warm". Lots of white space. Nothing loud:
no bright blocks of colour, no heavy shadows, no busy animation. The photos
do the talking.

## The moves (and how to do each one MDB's way)

| Bria does | Do it like this for MDB |
|---|---|
| **Portfolio grid of tall portrait photos** (about 2:3, ~393×590 at desktop, 3 across, generous gaps) | Portfolio / project grids: 3 columns of 2:3 photos, ~40px gaps; 1 column on phones |
| **White caption card overlapping the bottom of each photo**: project name in small spaced-out capitals, one-line description in italic serif underneath | Card overlaps the photo's bottom edge by ~20px, centred, white, no shadow. Name: grey `#53565A`, ~13px, uppercase, letter-spacing ~0.2em. Line under it: Playfair Display italic, ~14px |
| **Big light serif "statement" paragraph** centred on a warm cream band (`#F5F2EE`), ~40px, thin weight | A short MDB statement in Playfair Display, light, ~36–40px, grey, centred on a cream band. One per page, max |
| **Photo with a white text card overlapping its side** ("What sets us apart"): small spaced capitals heading, short paragraph, outlined button | Same layout: photo ~60% width, white card overlapping its right edge, heading in small spaced uppercase, body 15–16px, outlined button |
| **Outlined buttons**: thin double-line border, tiny spaced uppercase label, white background | MDB already uses double rules; outlined buttons in grey or teal fit. Keep the solid teal button for "Schedule your consultation" |
| **Handwritten script accent** ("get inspired") above a section | Optional, sparing: one short script word above a section. Needs a script font Josh/Summer approve |
| **Thin full-width hairlines** between sections, cream and white bands alternating | MDB's double hairlines already match this |
| **Centred logo header**, menu on the left, contact on the right, very quiet | Only if Summer asks; the header is shared by the whole site |
| **Small spaced-out capitals for labels everywhere** (nav, headings, buttons) | Matches MDB's spaced uppercase already |

## How to use this
- Apply it to what she asks about, not the whole site at once.
- Show the preview. If she's unsure, offer 2–3 small variations ("closer to
  Bria" / "more MDB") side by side rather than asking her to describe it.
- Service pages currently mirror the old WordPress look on purpose (Josh 10/1).
  Restyling them toward Bria is fine when Summer asks; say which pages it
  changes.
